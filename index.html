Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which e-commerce platforms do you specialise in?

A: We primarily focus on Amazon UK, eBay, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Etsy, and social commerce via Instagram and Facebook. We also provide guidance on EU marketplace expansion.

Q: What is your pricing structure?

A: We typically operate on a "base retainer + performance commission" model. This aligns our interests with yours—sharing both risks and rewards for optimal outcomes.

Q: Can you work with newly established businesses?

A: Absolutely. We offer a specialised "Launch Accelerator" programme covering everything from platform onboarding through to your first sale and beyond.

Q: How quickly can we expect to see results?

A: Following initial optimisation, most clients observe noticeable traffic improvements within the first month. Product launch campaigns typically require 1-3 months to reach full momentum.

Q: Do you provide services for international brands entering the UK market?

A: Yes, we specialise in UK market entry strategies including regulatory compliance, localisation, VAT registration guidance, and building relationships with UK logistics providers.
